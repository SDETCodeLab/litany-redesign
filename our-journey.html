Our Journey

Established as Individual Consultancy Litany Energy Services in Singapore Litany Energy Services Group has established a strong presence in the Asia-Pacific region has forged a reputation built on its hallmark traits of providing quality services, within budget, safely and on-time delivery.

What began as our first project has grown into a journey of transformation and success. Today, we stand as a regional partner with global expertise, driven by commitment to excellence, collaboration, and a relentless focus on exceptional project execution.

We aspire to be an excellent global engineering, procurement and constructor company working on project to align the individual objectives between various stakeholders while creating a smooth execution flow leading to value creation for ultimate customer.
